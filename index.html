Top three must-do activities in Vancouver

Explore Stanley Park and the Seawall

Walk, run, or rent a bicycle to loop around the 10-kilometer Stanley Park Seawall, which hugs the water with views of the ocean, downtown skyline, and North Shore Mountains.

Visit Granville Island Public Market

Take a short False Creek ferry or water taxi over to Granville Island and wander through the vibrant indoor Public Market.

Walk the Capilano Suspension Bridge

Cross the famous 137-meter-long, 70-meter-high suspension bridge swaying above the Capilano River, then explore the Treetops Adventure and Cliffwalk through the coastal rainforest.
